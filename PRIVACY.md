# Portside privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Portside is a Chrome extension that shows your tab groups in a sidebar, with pinned tabs, a command bar, and an archive of closed tabs.

## What Portside reads

To do that job, Portside reads:

- The titles, links, and icons of your open tabs, and which tab group each one is in.
- Your browsing history, only if you turn on "Search my browsing history too" in the command bar. Chrome asks for your permission first. It is off by default.

## What Portside saves

Portside saves these things in Chrome's extension storage on your computer:

- Your pinned tabs and favorites (title and link).
- Your folders, and the icon you picked for each tab group.
- The archive: the title, link, and close time of tabs you closed from the sidebar or that were archived for being unused. It keeps the latest 1,000.
- Your settings, such as text size and how long before unused tabs are archived.

## What Portside does not do

- It does not send any of this off your computer. Portside has no server.
- It does not use analytics, ads, or tracking.
- It does not sell or share your data with anyone.
- It does not load or run code from the internet.

The only network requests it causes are for the icons of sites you already have open, the same icons Chrome shows on its tabs.

## Deleting your data

- Empty the archive from the archive view in the sidebar.
- Remove a pinned tab or favorite by right-clicking it.
- Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it saved.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
